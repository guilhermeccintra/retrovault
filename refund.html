Our Guarantee

At RetroVault Magazines, we stand behind the quality of our digital collection. We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all purchases. If you are not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, you may request a full refund within 7 days of your purchase date.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, simply send an email to retrovaultmags@outlook.com with the following information:

  • Your full name
  • The email address used for the purchase
  • Your order number or transaction ID
  • The reason for your refund request (optional)

Refund Processing

  • Refund requests must be made within 7 calendar days of the original purchase date.
  • Once your refund request is received and approved, we will process the refund within 3-5 business days.
  • The refund will be credited back to your original payment method.
  • Depending on your bank or payment provider, it may take an additional 5-10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement.

Important Notes

  • Refund requests made after the 7-day period will not be accepted.
  • Once a refund is processed, your access to the digital content will be revoked.
  • We reserve the right to deny refund requests that show evidence of abuse or fraud.
